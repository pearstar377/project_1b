Cultures and Internet Culture

Memory and Information

The internet has not only made information faster. It has changed what people are expected to do with information. People may remember less of the information itself and more about where to find it again.

Verification used to be handled more visibly by institutions such as editors. Online, that work often falls to ordinary users.

The problem is not simply that technology has weakened attention or memory. The problem is that people now have a new responsibility that many were never trained to handle.
