Cultures and Internet Culture

This page looks at how online culture creates belonging through boundaries, memes, timing, and verification.

Main Idea

Internet culture is shaped by belonging, copying, timing, attention, and verification.

Memes work because they carry context. Subcultures protect meaning through boundaries. Platforms shape what spreads. Users must learn not only how to participate, but also how to question what they see.
