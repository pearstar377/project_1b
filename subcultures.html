Subcultures and Boundaries

A joke everyone understands is no longer much of an inside joke.

Culture is often described as something shared, but shared culture does not always mean open culture. Subcultures create meaning through difference. A symbol, phrase, style, or joke can feel powerful because not everyone understands it immediately.

Gatekeeping can be rude or exclusionary, but it can also work as self-protection when a community's style or language is taken, copied, or sold by outsiders.
